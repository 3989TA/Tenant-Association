NYC Resident Protection

Essential NYC Tenant Rights & Hotlines

Every tenant at 39-89 50th Street is entitled to safe, habitable conditions under NYC and NY State Housing laws.

NYC 311 / HPD Complaints

For lack of heat/hot water, unaddressed leaks, vermin, or dangerous conditions. Call 311 or submit online.

File HPD Report Online

NYS HCR Rent Oversight

Request your official rent history, verify rent stabilization status, or file rent reduction complaints.

Visit NYS HCR Portal

NYC Right to Counsel

Free legal representation for qualifying NYC tenants facing housing court or eviction actions.

Access Free Legal Aid

Housing Court Answers

Direct hotline answering tenant questions regarding rent laws, lease renewals, and tenant rights: (212) 962-4795.

Visit Housing Court Answers

Urgent Emergencies

For immediate hazards like gas leaks, call 911 or Con Edison immediately at 1-800-75-CONED (1-800-752-6633). For no heat or hot water during heat season, call 311.
